Goldpan

New addresses for your list. Only the gold gets in.

Goldpan is an email field for adding people to a list you already have: team members, subscribers, guests. Paste the new addresses from Excel, Outlook or anywhere: it spots who is already in, drops duplicates, fixes misspelled domains in one click and stops at the places left.

  1. The workspace already has members4 of its 12 seats are taken, and Goldpan knows who is already in.
  2. Invite and pastePress “Invite people” and paste a list, or use the example.
  3. Check the colours, then inviteGreen and yellow will be invited. Red ones can be fixed, or left out with “Ignore and send”.
  • No dependencies
  • ~10 kB gzipped
  • TypeScript types
  • Accessible
  • 8 languages

The three colours

Three colours, one rule: only red is left out

As soon as you paste them, the addresses take a colour. Green and yellow ones will be invited, red ones will not. Yellow is only a warning: worth a look before you send.

Valid

Will be invited.

  • Written correctly
  • On a domain that is not blocked
  • Not registered yet, and there is room

To check

Will be invited, with a warning.

  • Maybe a typo (hotmial.it): “Fix” corrects it in one click
  • Already registered: members, or people already invited
  • Flagged by a check you added

Rejected

Left out.

  • Not email addresses (no domain, a space…)
  • Blocked domain: throw-away mailboxes, or misspelled domains like gmal.com (with “Fix”)
  • No free seats left

An address pasted twice is ignored: the first one stays and lights up for a moment.

Playground

Change a setting and watch the colours change

Each setting is an option of the library: press ⓘ to see what it does. The code below follows your choices.

maxEmails

How many addresses can still be accepted, green and yellow together. Usually the free seats of your list. Tick “no limit” to remove it.

onLimit

The addresses that do not fit are shown in red (“No place left”) or not added at all. When a seat frees up, the first red one waiting takes it.

duplicates

Ignore: the copy is not added and the first one lights up. Yellow: both stay and each takes a seat (e.g. two tickets for the same person). Red: the copy stays in the list as rejected.

existing

What happens to the addresses already in your list (see “Addresses already registered” below). Yellow: invited, with a note. Red: rejected.

typoCheck

Spots domains with a typo compared to a popular provider (hotmial.it → hotmail.it) and offers a “Fix” button. Real domains such as hotmail.be or aon.com are left alone. Yellow only warns; red rejects the address until it is fixed.

editable

Which addresses show the pencil, to correct them in place. The ✕ to remove them is always there.

rejected

What happens when you press “Invite” while red addresses are present. Warn: the first press shows them with a message, and the button turns into “Ignore and send”; the second press invites the others. Block: they are shown, and nobody is invited until they are fixed or removed. No warning: the others are invited straight away.

locale

Language of the texts in the field: filters, messages, buttons. 8 languages are included and more can be added.

theme

Light, dark or automatic (follows the setting of the device). Every colour can also be changed with CSS variables.

existingEmails

The people already in your list (members, earlier invites): they turn yellow with the note “Already registered”. On a real page they come from your data; here you can type them, separated by commas.

blockedDomains

Addresses on these domains are always red. They add to the domains blocked out of the box: misspelled domains like gmal.com and throw-away mailboxes like mailinator.com. *.domain.com also blocks its sub-domains.

      Plain HTML form
      
    Also works on an ordinary form field
    
      
    No list, no popup: it attaches to a field you already have. The field stays in the form and always holds the accepted addresses, ready to be sent. With red addresses, the first press shows them and the button turns into “Ignore and send”. A required field is never sent empty.

      What it does
      
    Built for real lists, the copied-and-pasted kind
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Starts from your data
    It knows who is already registered and how many seats are left.
    
      
    Paste from anywhere
    An Excel column, an Outlook line with names, a list with commas or semicolons: it reads them all.
    
      
    Fixes typos
    It suggests gmail.com for gmial.com in one click, and leaves real domains such as hotmail.be or aon.com alone.
    
      
    Keeps unwanted domains out
    Throw-away mailboxes, and the domains you choose, are rejected.
    
      
    Respects the seats
    It never accepts more addresses than there are free seats. Remove one, and the first address left out takes its place.
    
      
    No double invites
    An address pasted twice is ignored and the first one lights up: nobody gets two emails.
    
      
    Usable by everyone
    It works with the keyboard alone, with screen readers, with Chinese and Japanese keyboards and in high-contrast mode.
    
      
    8 languages
    English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese. Adding one takes a single file.
    
      
    Fits your site
    Colours change with CSS variables, light or dark follows the device, and it works with React, Vue or plain HTML.
    
    
    
    
    For developers: one line of code to attach it to an existing field, about 10 kB, no other libraries, TypeScript types and events. Pasted text is never run as code.
